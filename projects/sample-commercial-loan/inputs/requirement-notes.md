# Commercial loan policy check: kickoff notes

Sample input with made-up data.

## §1 Today

Relationship managers (RMs) fill in an Excel checklist for every commercial loan application. Credit staff check the same checklist again by hand. It is slow, the results vary between people, and it is hard to show regulators how a decision was reached.

## §2 Policy rules

The system must check these rules when an RM submits an application:

- Debt service coverage ratio (DSCR) at least 1.25x.
- Loan to value (LTV) at most 75% when real estate is the collateral.
- Total exposure to one borrower at most $10M, counting the borrower's existing loans.
- Risk rating 1 to 6 is eligible. A risk rating of 7 or worse is declined.

## §3 Exceptions

Any failed rule needs an exception decision before the loan can go ahead. A credit officer decides exceptions. Above $5M a senior credit officer decides.

## §4 Records and access

Keep a full record of every check and every decision for regulators. Only RMs and credit staff may see check results, because they include borrowers' financial details.
