# Mandatory checklist for the BRD

Each line is one topic the BRD must cover, followed by the words that count as covering it.
Edit this list to match your organisation. The BRD cannot be approved until each topic is covered or waived by the reviewer.

- Security and access: security, access, authoris, authoriz, permission, role
- Personal data and privacy: personal data, privacy, pii, confidential
- Data retention: retention, retain, kept for, keep for, archive
- Accessibility: accessibility, wcag, screen reader
- Audit trail: audit, records of, record of, record-keeping, logged
- Performance: performance, response time, within
